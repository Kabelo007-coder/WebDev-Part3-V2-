About Kay's Clothing Co.

Kay's Clothing Co. was founded to solve problems for companies in need of uniforms. The founder saw firsthand while electricians were working on residential and commercial sites that the standard workwear was not built for the job. Off-the-shelf uniform tore easier and lacked proper pocket sizes for tools(pliers and testers) and offered little protection against flash skin and static risks. We started with small production of trousers and shirts for local electrical contracting firms. The company grew into a dedicated supplier of uniforms and workwear for electricians, now serving independent tradespeople and smaller contracting businesses.

What we stand for

Aim

To be trusted uniform suppliers of choice for electrical trade businesses, known for combining safety compliance, comfort and a professional brand image in the industry.

To equip electricians with durable, safe and professional-looking uniforms that perform as hard as the people who wear them.

Our target market

  1. We target independent electricians and small electrical contracting businesses (5-20 staff) looking for durable, professional uniforms.
  2. Facilities management and building companies needing bulk uniform orders with consistent branding across teams.
  3. Procurement and operations managers responsible for ordering and re-ordering staff uniforms on a recurring basis.

Our goals

  1. Produce qualified trade leads from electrical contracting businesses looking to outfit their teams.
  2. Enable direct online communication (ordering and re-ordering of uniforms).
  3. Move from workwear retailer to a specialist of workwear through the contents of safety standards relevant to electricians.
  4. Provide a straightforward way for businesses to request custom branding (logos and company names) on uniforms.
